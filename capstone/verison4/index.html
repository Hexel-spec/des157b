Hello, and welcome to the beta test for Seabnb, the online rating platform for floating cities! I'm Savvy the Sea Turtle, and I'll be assisting you on your journey today!

In the near future, sea levels will rise due to climate change and cause many coastal areas to become inhabitable, displacing a multitude of coastal communities.

Floating city projects have emerged as an answer to this crisis, claiming to allow those that have been displaced (and anyone else who is interest) a safe and comfortable home.

Seabnb aims to allow people to make informed decisions about living in floating cities, which is why we're asking you to explore these floating cities and form your own conclusions

You've been tasked with visiting three different floating cities: Maldives Floating City in Maldvies, Oceanix Busan in South Korea, and Dogen City in Japan. We wish you good luck on your journeys!
