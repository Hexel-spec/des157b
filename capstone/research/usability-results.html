Usability Results

3 significant observations

  1. My user testers had a hard time locating the confirmation prompt after clicking the map pin.
  2. Sometimes my user testers would kind of just breeze through the text/questions. Maybe the text alone isn’t really capturing their attention.
  3. One user tester went to Done Exploring early out of curiosity. I should work on making users explore all the cities before it appears as an option

Aspects to be improved

  • Move the confirmation message/buttons are too far away from the map pins to notice
  • Change the speech bubble color to something other than grey
  • Text alone might be hard to understand

Can users figure out how to navigate/interact with your project?

Users don't really struggle with navigating through the project; it's pretty straightforward for the most part. The only thing they really struggled with was noticing the confirmation message after clicking a map pin.

Does the project impact the user the way you want it to?

It's easy for them to understand on a basic level, but the user testers' final decisions didn't have much thought behind them for the most part. I should expand the site's features to prompt deeper thinking.

What modifications need to be made to interactions, visuals or other elements of the project to make it stronger?

  • Move confimation buttons closer to map pins
  • Replace placeholder images with custom images and have them change for each question.
  • Make the speech bubble disappear when clicking ‘no further questions’, and change its color

  1. Finish written content for Dogen City, and code interactions.
  2. Develop content for the form at the end/ending message.
  3. Make illustrations for each city, at least 2 for each.
